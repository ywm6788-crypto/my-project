# Figure caption

**Figure X. Test performance and descriptive spectral structure across six EEG motor-classification tasks.** (a) Test accuracy with trial-level bootstrap 95% confidence intervals; the red dashed marker denotes the best accuracy among the 15 reference models. These intervals are not subject-clustered. (b) For PhysioNet tasks 1–4, scalp maps show the within-subject difference in 8–30 Hz log power between annotation events T1 and T2, averaged with equal weight across 22 test subjects. Maps include the 15 motor-area electrodes used by the model; electrode positions are schematic and Gaussian-kernel interpolation is restricted to the electrodes’ convex hull. The BCI IIIA bars show the 12 channels with the largest mean five-band standard deviation of class-specific mean log power, normalized to the maximum. The BCI IIIB bars show the class 0 minus class 1 log-power contrast for the two channels in stored order; electrode coordinates were unavailable. (c) Test-accuracy differences in percentage points relative to the previous selected method (x-axis) and the best of 15 historical reference models (y-axis). The unweighted mean change relative to the previous round was +1.06 percentage points. Historical model predictions were unavailable for paired testing. (d) Separate t-SNE embeddings of standardized per-channel log-power features in five frequency bands (8–12, 12–16, 16–20, 20–24, and 24–30 Hz). PhysioNet plots use a seeded, class-balanced sample of 120 test trials per class; BCI plots use all test trials. Perplexity was 25, except for BCI IIIA (16). Colors indicate true class labels. t-SNE coordinates and inter-cluster distances are not quantitative.

The fixed test partitions had been examined in earlier experiment rounds; results are exploratory. Trial bootstrap intervals do not account for within-subject dependence.
